# AFW-DLV-0016.4 - AML Transaction Monitoring Engine

## Objective

Provide deterministic sandbox transaction-monitoring signals and alerts.

## Responsibilities

- large-amount signals
- velocity signals
- structuring signals
- geographic-risk signals
- repeated-beneficiary signals
- deterministic transaction risk score
- alert creation
- audit trail
- false-positive workflow foundation

## Risk bands

- LOW
- MEDIUM
- HIGH
- CRITICAL

## Architectural boundary

AFW-DLV-0016.4 produces monitoring signals and alerts. It does not submit regulatory reports, file SAR/STR reports, suspend accounts automatically, make legal determinations, replace sanctions/PEP screening or perform external AML certification.

## Sandbox policy

Thresholds, geographic indicators and rules in this delivery are development and sandbox policy values. They are not production regulatory thresholds.

## Production activation

Production monitoring policies require jurisdiction-specific compliance review, legal approval, calibrated thresholds, production transaction datasets, formal validation and controlled deployment.

## Decision

Successful validation means: READY FOR REVIEW

It does not mean:

- AML CERTIFIED
- REGULATORY APPROVED
- PRODUCTION MONITORING APPROVED
