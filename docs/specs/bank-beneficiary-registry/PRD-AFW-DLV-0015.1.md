# AFW-DLV-0015.1 — Bank Account & Beneficiary Registry

## Objective

Provide the canonical Banking Platform registry for external bank beneficiaries and their bank account identifiers.

## Responsibilities

- beneficiary creation
- external bank account registration
- account ownership context
- IBAN/account-number normalization
- duplicate prevention
- verification lifecycle
- account masking
- privacy-safe API projections
- audit foundation
- telemetry foundation

## Out of scope

- bank transfer execution
- bank routing
- bank settlement
- direct bank API integration
- ownership certification by a real bank

Those capabilities belong to later Sprint 15 deliveries.
