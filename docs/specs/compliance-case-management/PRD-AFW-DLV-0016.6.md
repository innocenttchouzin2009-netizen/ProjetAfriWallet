# AFW-DLV-0016.6 - Compliance Case Management Platform

Consumes references from sanctions/PEP screening, AML monitoring, financial risk scoring, and authorized manual escalation.

## Responsibilities

Case creation, source linking, priority, assignment, notes, escalation, resolution, false-positive disposition, operational restriction disposition, closure, and immutable audit trail.

## Boundary

The platform does not duplicate screening, transaction monitoring, or risk scoring. Case decisions are internal workflow decisions, not criminal findings, legal determinations, regulatory filings, sanctions determinations, or AML certification.

Regulatory filing is not implemented. Successful validation means READY FOR REVIEW.
