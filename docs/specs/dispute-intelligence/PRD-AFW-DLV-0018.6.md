# AFW-DLV-0018.6

## Customer Protection & Dispute Intelligence Engine

### Objective

Produce deterministic and explainable dispute-intelligence findings from normalized data generated by AFW-DLV-0018.1 through 0018.5.

### Inputs

- Dispute claims
- eligibility results
- investigation outcomes
- refund/chargeback decisions
- resolution orchestration results

### Capabilities

- repeated-claim detection
- merchant dispute concentration
- beneficiary dispute concentration
- favorable decision concentration
- failed resolution detection
- resolution-time metrics
- compound protection-risk detection
- protection score
- severity
- operational recommendation
- audit trail

### Recommendations

- NoAction
- Monitor
- CustomerProtectionReview
- ReviewMerchant
- EscalateOperations

### Explainability

Every score contribution must be represented by an explicit `ProtectionPattern`.

### Critical boundary

Recommendations are analytical outputs. This delivery does not:

- block merchants
- suspend customers
- freeze wallets
- execute refunds
- execute chargebacks
- move money
- modify Universal Ledger

### Validation

Successful validation means: `READY FOR REVIEW`
