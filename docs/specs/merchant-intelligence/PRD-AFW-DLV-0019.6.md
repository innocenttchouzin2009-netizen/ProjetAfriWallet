# AFW-DLV-0019.6

Produce deterministic, explainable merchant-risk findings from Merchant Platform activity: checkout, authorization decisions, settlement/payout orchestration, dispute references and external risk snapshots.

Capabilities include rate metrics, risk patterns, score, severity and protection recommendations. Recommendations are analytical only: no merchant block/suspension, settlement/payout freeze, capture, money movement or Universal Ledger mutation occurs.
