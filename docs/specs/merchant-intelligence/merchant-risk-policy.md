# Merchant Risk Policy - Sandbox v1

Patterns: decline rate >=40% (minimum 3 decisions), step-up rate >=30% (minimum 3), risk score >=85, failed/manual-intervention settlements, at least 2 additional settlement attempts, at least 3 disputes, at least 2 refund/chargeback recommendations, at least 100 checkouts, and compound risk from 3 patterns.

Severity: 0-9 Informational, 10-29 Low, 30-59 Medium, 60-79 High, 80-100 Critical. These are internal sandbox thresholds.
