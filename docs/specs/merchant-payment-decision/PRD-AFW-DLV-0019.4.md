# AFW-DLV-0019.4

Transform `ReadyForAuthorization` Payment Intents into deterministic, versioned and explainable merchant-payment decisions: Authorize, Decline, RequiresStepUp or CaptureEligible.

This delivery introduces no real authorization, processor call, wallet debit, capture, settlement, payout, money movement or Universal Ledger mutation. `Authorize` and `CaptureEligible` are recommendations only.
