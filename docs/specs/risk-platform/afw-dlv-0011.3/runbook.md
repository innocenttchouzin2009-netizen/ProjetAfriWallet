# Runbook

## Incident response
- Confirm the transaction identifier and AWID in the request.
- Review the evaluation result, decision, and factor contributions.
- Escalate to the fraud operations team if the decision is block or manual review with high-risk factors.

## Recovery
- Re-run the scenario harness to verify the engine behavior after any threshold change.
- Redeploy the service if the build or scenario validation fails.
