# Investigator Guide — AFW-DLV-0011.5

## Purpose
Guide compliance investigators through the case lifecycle and required evidence practices.

## Workflow
1. Validate alert context (source, type, severity).
2. Confirm assignment ownership.
3. Attach evidence with clear labels and content references.
4. Add investigation notes with factual statements.
5. Escalate when required by policy.
6. Record final decision and rationale.
7. Close case only after resolution checks.

## Required Artifacts
- Source alert details
- Session and transaction evidence
- Investigator notes
- Final case decision rationale

## Operational Rules
- Never remove prior notes.
- Use concise, verifiable evidence statements.
- Escalate on cross-border anomalies and critical risk indicators.
