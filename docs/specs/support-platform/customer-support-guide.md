# Customer Support Guide — AFW-DLV-0012.2

## Process
1. Create case from customer channel.
2. Confirm assignment to the right support team.
3. Communicate with customer through support messages.
4. Track SLA state and respond before warning thresholds.
5. Resolve and close only after customer confirmation.

## Do Not
- Expose internal notes to customers.
- Attach unsupported file types.
